# Bagian I — Teori Esensial Pemanasan Global

## A. Apa itu Pemanasan Global?

**Pemanasan global (global warming)** = kenaikan suhu rata-rata permukaan Bumi akibat akumulasi **gas rumah kaca (greenhouse gases / GHG)** di atmosfer. Bumi sudah naik ~1.1-1.3°C sejak pre-industrial (~1850).

> 💡 **Perubahan iklim (climate change)** = istilah lebih luas: kenaikan suhu + perubahan pola cuaca + dampak terkait.

## B. Efek Rumah Kaca (Greenhouse Effect)

**Efek rumah kaca** = proses dimana **atmosfer Bumi menjebak panas** dari Matahari. Tanpa efek rumah kaca, Bumi -18°C (uninhabitable). Dengan efek rumah kaca alami → +15°C avg (life supporting).

### Mekanisme:
1. Sinar matahari (UV + visible) sampai Bumi.
2. Bumi serap → panas → memancarkan kembali sebagai radiasi IR.
3. GHG di atmosfer **menyerap IR** → tidak lepas ke ruang angkasa.
4. Panas terjebak → suhu Bumi naik.

### "Rumah Kaca" Analogi:
- Kaca rumah kaca → transparan untuk visible light (masuk).
- Kaca → block IR (panas trapped).
- Atmosfer GHG = mirip kaca rumah kaca.

## C. Gas Rumah Kaca (GHG)

### GHG Utama:
1. **Karbon dioksida (CO₂)** — paling banyak; dari pembakaran fosil; pre-industrial 280 ppm → sekarang 420 ppm.
2. **Methane (CH₄)** — 28× lebih potent dari CO₂ (per molekul, 100-tahun); dari sapi, lahan basah, fossil fuels.
3. **Nitrous oxide (N₂O)** — 273× lebih potent; dari pertanian (pupuk).
4. **Water vapor (H₂O)** — natural greenhouse gas; feedback (warming → more humidity).
5. **Fluorinated gases** — synthetic (CFC, HFC); sangat potent (thousands of CO₂).
6. **Ozone permukaan** — short-lived but warming.

### Greenhouse vs Pelindung:
- **Atmosfer GHG** = menjaga suhu Bumi → essential.
- **Lapisan ozon stratosfer** = melindungi dari UV (beda gas).

## D. Penyebab Pemanasan Global

### Aktivitas Manusia (Anthropogenic):
1. **Pembakaran fosil fuel** (~75% CO₂): bensin, batu bara, gas alam — power, transport, industri.
2. **Deforestasi & land use change** (~25% emissions): hilangnya carbon sink + carbon release.
3. **Pertanian intensif** — methane (sapi, sawah), N₂O (pupuk).
4. **Industri** — semen production CO₂, refrigerants.
5. **Limbah** — landfill methane.

### Alami (kecil):
- Volcanic activity.
- Solar variability.
- Natural climate cycles.

> 💡 IPCC consensus >99%: pemanasan global modern dominantly anthropogenic.

## E. Bukti Pemanasan Global

1. **Suhu rata-rata naik** ~1.1-1.3°C sejak 1850.
2. **Sea level rise** ~20 cm sejak 1900.
3. **Gletser & ice sheets melting** (Greenland, Antarctica, Himalaya).
4. **Arctic sea ice declining** dramatically.
5. **Ocean warming** (90% extra heat goes to ocean).
6. **Extreme weather** lebih sering (badai, kekeringan, banjir).
7. **Coral bleaching** events.
8. **Phenology shifts** (musim semi datang lebih awal).
9. **Spesies migrasi** ke kutub/altitude tinggi.

## F. Dampak Pemanasan Global

### Lingkungan:
- **Iklim ekstrem**: hurricane lebih kuat, drought intense, heatwave.
- **Ekosistem rusak**: coral bleaching, forest fires, species extinction.
- **Sea level rise**: coastal flooding, salt water intrusion.
- **Ice melting**: positive feedback (less reflection).

### Manusia:
- **Food security**: crop failures, hunger.
- **Water security**: drought, sungai mengering.
- **Health**: heat stress, disease shift (malaria, dengue), pollution.
- **Migration**: climate refugees.
- **Ekonomi**: damage billions, infrastructure rusak.

### Indonesia:
- Sea level rise → ancam pesisir & pulau-pulau.
- Coral reefs Coral Triangle terancam.
- Cuaca ekstrem → banjir + kekeringan.
- Karhutla lebih frequent → asap regional.
- Pertanian terganggu.

## G. Aksi Mitigasi

### Mitigasi (Reduce emissions):
1. **Renewable energy** (solar, wind, hydro, geothermal).
2. **Efisiensi energi** (LED, EV, building efficiency).
3. **Reforestasi** & forest protection.
4. **Sustainable agriculture** (no-till, reduce livestock).
5. **Industri & transport** decarbonization.
6. **Carbon capture** & storage.
7. **Kebijakan**: carbon tax, cap-and-trade, subsidi renewable.

### Adaptasi (Prepare for impacts):
1. **Sea walls** & coastal protection.
2. **Drought-resistant crops**.
3. **Better drainage & flood control**.
4. **Heat-resistant infrastructure**.
5. **Early warning systems**.
6. **Climate-smart agriculture**.
7. **Health system preparation**.

## H. Komitmen Global & Indonesia

### Global:
- **UNFCCC** (1992) — UN climate framework.
- **Kyoto Protocol** (1997) — first binding agreement.
- **Paris Agreement** (2015) — limit warming <2°C, aim 1.5°C.
- **COP** annual summits — latest negotiations.

### Indonesia:
- **NDC**: reduce GHG 31.89% by 2030 (unconditional), 43.20% conditional.
- **Net Zero** by 2060 (or earlier).
- **FOLU Net Sink** 2030 (forestry & land use carbon-neutral).
- **JETP** $20B+ partnership untuk energy transition.

## 💡 Konsep Inti yang Wajib Dikuasai

1. **Pemanasan global** = kenaikan suhu Bumi karena GHG manusia.
2. **Efek rumah kaca alami** essential; **enhanced** by humans = problem.
3. **GHG utama**: CO₂ (paling banyak), CH₄ (potent), N₂O, H₂O, F-gases.
4. **Sebab** anthropogenic: fossil fuels, deforestasi, pertanian, industri.
5. **Bukti**: suhu naik 1.1-1.3°C, sea level, ice melt, extreme weather.
6. **Paris Agreement**: target <1.5°C; net zero by 2050.
7. **Indonesia NDC**: 31-43% reduction by 2030, net zero 2060.
8. **Aksi**: mitigasi (reduce emissions) + adaptasi (prepare impacts).
