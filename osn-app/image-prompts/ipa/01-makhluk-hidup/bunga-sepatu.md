Orientation: FRONT = looking into the flower face; BACK = calyx side; LEFT/RIGHT = side profile. Every panel shows the full body, nothing cropped. Straight-on, orthographic, no three-quarter angles. No text labels, no panel borders, no dividing lines; the whole sheet sits on one continuous pure white background.
